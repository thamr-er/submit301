Task 1 — FCFS CPU Scheduling

What is FCFS?

First-Come, First-Served (FCFS) is the simplest CPU scheduling algorithm. Processes are executed in the order they arrive in the ready queue. The CPU runs each process to completion before moving to the next one (non-preemptive).


Think of it like a queue at a ticket counter — whoever arrives first gets served first, no matter how long they take.

Key Terms

  • Arrival Time (AT) — when the process enters the ready queue
  • Burst Time (BT) — how much CPU time the process needs
  • Completion Time (CT) — when the process finishes
  • Turnaround Time (TAT) = CT − AT
  • Waiting Time (WT) = TAT − BT

Advantages & Disadvantages

✅ Pros
  • Very simple to implement
  • No starvation — every process runs
  • Easy to understand
❌ Cons
  • Convoy Effect — short jobs wait behind long ones
  • High average waiting time
  • Not good for interactive systems
